# Code of conduct

Anyone taking part in the project's issues, reviews, discussions or events should be able to contribute without harassment. Critique the design or behavior under review. Explain disagreements with evidence and give contributors room to correct mistakes.

Personal attacks, discriminatory comments, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. The same standard applies when representing the project in public.

Report an incident privately to a project maintainer. Include the location, approximate time and relevant messages. For abuse on GitHub, including an incident involving a maintainer, you can also [report the content directly to GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Maintainers will handle reports discreetly, hear the people involved and limit access to those needed to resolve the incident. Depending on severity and repeated behavior, they may remove content, issue a warning or suspend participation.
